# France — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

A perennial powerhouse with arguably the deepest talent pool in world football. France combine athletic, vertical attacking with elite individual match-winners.

## Playing style
Direct and transition-heavy: absorb pressure, break at speed through pace out wide and a clinical central striker. Can flip a match in moments.

## Key players
- Kylian Mbappé (FW) — generational pace and finishing; the team's gravitational center.
- Aurélien Tchouaméni (MF) — shielding midfielder enabling the front line.
- Ousmane Dembélé (FW) — unpredictable wide threat.

## Recent form
Comfortable UEFA qualifying; occasional complacency against compact defenses. Squad depth lets them rotate deep into a tournament.

## Notable head-to-head
France vs Argentina: the 2022 final (3–3, lost on penalties) still frames the rivalry. France vs Brazil remains a marquee heavyweight clash.
