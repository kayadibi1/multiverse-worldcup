# England — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

Blessed with one of the deepest attacking talent pools in the world, England arrive as genuine contenders carrying the familiar weight of expectation and the search for a first World Cup since 1966.

## Playing style
Solid defensive base with elite individual attackers. Sometimes cautious in big games; their ceiling depends on whether they unleash the front line.

## Key players
- Jude Bellingham (MF) — box-to-box engine and big-game scorer.
- Harry Kane (FW) — prolific striker and creative focal point.
- Bukayo Saka (FW) — incisive wide threat.

## Recent form
Strong qualifying record; questions persist about midfield control against elite opposition.

## Notable head-to-head
England vs France and England vs Spain are the knockout-stage tests that have historically exposed or vindicated them.
