# Argentina — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

Reigning world champions, Argentina blend tournament savvy with a settled, confident spine. They control tempo, defend deep when needed, and are ruthless in knockout moments.

## Playing style
Pragmatic, game-state aware: patient build-up, compact mid-block, lethal on set pieces and in transition. Comfortable in low-scoring, high-pressure matches.

## Key players
- Lionel Messi (FW) — talisman; his availability and minutes shape the ceiling.
- Julián Álvarez (FW) — relentless pressing forward, decisive in big games.
- Enzo Fernández (MF) — metronome who dictates rhythm.

## Recent form
Strong CONMEBOL campaign; proven ability to win ugly. Depth questions if Messi is rested or injured.

## Notable head-to-head
Argentina vs France: 2022 final won on penalties after a 3–3 classic — a rivalry with edge and history.
