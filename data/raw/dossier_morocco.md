# Morocco — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

The story of 2022 — Africa's first World Cup semifinalists — Morocco are no longer a surprise. They are a disciplined, fearless side that has beaten elite nations and believes it belongs.

## Playing style
Superbly organized defensively: compact, aggressive pressing in spells, lethal on the counter and from wide overloads. A nightmare matchup for possession-heavy favorites.

## Key players
- Achraf Hakimi (DF) — marauding attacking full-back and leader.
- Youssef En-Nesyri (FW) — aerial and penalty-box threat.
- Sofyan Amrabat (MF) — tireless defensive midfielder.

## Recent form
Carried 2022 momentum through qualifying; proven giant-killers with a settled identity.

## Notable head-to-head
Morocco stunned Spain (on penalties) and Portugal at the 2022 World Cup — evidence the favorites fear them.
