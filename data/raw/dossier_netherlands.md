# Netherlands — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

A well-organized, tactically flexible side that blends a commanding defensive core with bright attacking talent. The Dutch are perennial dark-horse finalists.

## Playing style
Structured and adaptable: comfortable in possession or on the counter, strong in defensive transitions, dangerous from set pieces.

## Key players
- Virgil van Dijk (DF) — commanding leader who anchors the back line.
- Cody Gakpo (FW) — versatile, productive forward.
- Frenkie de Jong (MF) — line-breaking carrier who links defense and attack.

## Recent form
Reliable UEFA qualifying; capable of upsetting any favorite when their structure holds.

## Notable head-to-head
Netherlands vs Argentina has produced fierce, ill-tempered knockout ties — a rivalry with real edge.
