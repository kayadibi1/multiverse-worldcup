# United States — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026, co-host)

As co-hosts on home soil, the USA carry unprecedented expectation and the energy of a young, athletic, European-based core. Home advantage and crowd support are real tournament factors.

## Playing style
High-energy, athletic, transition-oriented: aggressive pressing and quick vertical attacks. Game management against elite sides is the developmental question.

## Key players
- Christian Pulisic (FW) — talisman and primary creator.
- Weston McKennie (MF) — combative box-to-box presence.
- Gio Reyna (MF) — creative spark off the line.

## Recent form
Buoyed by host status and a favorable environment; the deepest American talent pool in history.

## Notable head-to-head
USA vs Mexico (the CONCACAF clásico) is the continent's fiercest rivalry; a deep run on home soil would be historic.
