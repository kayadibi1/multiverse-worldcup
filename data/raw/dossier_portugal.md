# Portugal — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

A golden generation deep in talent across every line, Portugal pair flair with newfound balance, capable of beating anyone on their day.

## Playing style
Flexible and technical: control midfield, attack through dynamic wide players and late-arriving runners. Defensive concentration in tight games is the swing factor.

## Key players
- Bruno Fernandes (MF) — creative hub and set-piece threat.
- Rafael Leão (FW) — explosive, direct wide runner.
- Vitinha (MF) — press-resistant controller.

## Recent form
Comfortable qualifying; squad depth allows them to absorb injuries. Knockout temperament is the open question.

## Notable head-to-head
Portugal vs Spain (the Iberian derby) and Portugal vs Brazil are the standout fixtures whenever the draw aligns.
