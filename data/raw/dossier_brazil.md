# Brazil — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

Brazil arrive as five-time world champions and perennial favorites, blending elite individual talent with a fluid, possession-based attack. Under a settled coaching setup, they press high and rely on devastating wide play.

## Playing style
High-tempo attacking football built around dribbling wingers and overlapping full-backs. Vulnerable in transition when the midfield is bypassed.

## Key players
- Vinícius Júnior (FW) — left-wing talisman, the team's primary creative threat.
- Rodrygo (FW) — versatile forward, decisive in knockout games.
- Casemiro (MF) — defensive anchor; his fitness is pivotal to balance.

## Recent form
Strong CONMEBOL qualifying campaign; occasional defensive lapses against organized, counter-attacking sides.

## Notable head-to-head
Brazil vs Nigeria: last competitive meeting finished 1–1. Nigeria's pace on the break has historically troubled Brazil's high line.
