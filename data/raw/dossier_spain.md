# Spain — Team Dossier (FIFA World Cup 2026)

Top of the Elo table and arguably the most cohesive team in the field, Spain dominate possession and suffocate opponents with relentless positional play and a golden generation of young talent.

## Playing style
Possession-based positional play: high line, quick combinations, overloads in wide areas. Can struggle to break down ultra-compact low blocks but rarely loses control of a match.

## Key players
- Rodri (MF) — the best holding midfielder in the world; his fitness is decisive.
- Lamine Yamal (FW) — precocious wide creator who unlocks packed defenses.
- Pedri (MF) — silky progressor who sets the tempo.

## Recent form
Imperious in UEFA qualifying; recent major-tournament pedigree restored. Depth and balance across the pitch.

## Notable head-to-head
Spain vs Germany and Spain vs France are the marquee European heavyweight clashes likely to decide their half of the bracket.
